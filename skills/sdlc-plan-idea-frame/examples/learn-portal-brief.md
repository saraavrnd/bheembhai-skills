# Idea Brief — Learn Portal

**Status:** Draft · **Date:** 2026-06-18 · **Next step:** `sdlc-plan-prd-write`

## 1. Idea as given
"An AI tutor for K-12 students that never just gives the answer. It talks to the student in
plain language, figures out where they're stuck, and walks them through it using the teacher's
own materials. Teachers get a view of who is struggling without reading every chat. Districts
need it to be safe and compliant. We'll run it locally in Docker first."

## 2. Problem
K-12 classes move at one pace, so a student who hits a conceptual roadblock falls behind and the
gap compounds. Students often can't name their confusion in academic terms, so static material
doesn't help and teachers find out too late, usually at the next assessment.

## 3. Target users
| User type | What they need | Why they matter to the PRD |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------------|
| Student | Get unstuck at their own pace, in plain language | The tutoring experience is the product |
| Teacher | See who is struggling and on what, without reading chats | Supplies materials; owns the dashboard feature area |
| District admin | Adopt it safely and compliantly | Drives the compliance and data-governance NFRs |

## 4. Value hypothesis
If students can get guided, source-grounded help the moment they are stuck, then their quiz
scores improve measurably and teachers spend fewer hours on reactive one-on-one support.

## 5. Assumptions
| ID | Assumption | Basis | Why |
|-----|------------|-------|-----|
| A-1 | The tutor never reveals a final answer, even when asked directly | Stated | "never just gives the answer" |
| A-2 | Teachers supply the curriculum; the product ships none | Inferred | "using the teacher's own materials" |
| A-3 | Students are provisioned from a school roster, not self sign-up | Inferred | K-12 students are minors and belong to a class |
| A-4 | First deployment is local Docker; cloud comes later | Stated | "run it locally in Docker first" |

## 6. Constraints
- Users include children under 13: COPPA and FERPA apply (inferred from "K-12" and "compliant").
- Local Docker deployment for the first release.
- Teachers must not need to read raw chat logs to get value.

## 7. Non-goals
- Supplying curriculum content.
- Grading or replacing assessments.
- Parent-facing features in the first release.
